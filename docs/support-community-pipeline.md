# Support + Community Pipeline

Phase 13 Track G support operations baseline.

## Pipeline Stages

1. Intake:
- issue templates for operators, integrators, and runtime incidents.
2. Triage:
- classify severity (`P1`, `P2`, `P3`) and assign owner.
3. Resolution:
- publish fix/workaround and expected follow-up.
4. Knowledge capture:
- update runbooks/quickstarts/examples after resolved issues.

## Ownership

- Support Lead: `Partner Support Lead`
- Runtime Owner: `Runtime Operations Lead`
- Chain Owner: `Chain Operations Lead`
- Integrator Support Owner: `Integrator Success Lead`

## SLA Targets

- P1 acknowledgement: <= 1 hour
- P2 acknowledgement: <= 1 business day
- P3 acknowledgement: <= 3 business days

## Escalation Routing

- Primary queue: `support@clawchain.ops`
- P1 hotline: `+1-555-0100`
- Integrator escalation channel: `#clawd-integrator-support`
